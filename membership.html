Join MentorBridge

Two paths. One community. Choose your role below and apply today — it's free and takes 5 minutes.

Choose Your Path

🧭 Become a Mentor

Mentors are experienced students or graduates who volunteer their time to guide others. You'll gain leadership skills, build your resume, and make a real difference.

  • Must be a sophomore, junior, senior, or grad student
  • Minimum 3.0 GPA recommended
  • Commit to 2–3 hours per week
  • Attend at least 2 club events per semester
  • Complete a short mentor training session
Apply as Mentor ↓

🌱 Become a Mentee

Mentees are students who want to grow — in any area of life. No prior experience needed. Just a genuine desire to improve and show up consistently.

  • Open to all years and majors
  • No GPA requirement
  • Be willing to meet weekly with your mentor
  • Set at least one personal growth goal
  • Participate in at least one club event
Apply as Mentee ↓

What Happens After You Apply

📝

1. Submit Your Form

Fill out the application below. It takes about 5 minutes. Tell us your goals and what areas you want to grow in.

📞

2. Short Interview

A club officer will reach out within 3–5 days for a quick 15-minute chat to learn more about you.

🤝

3. Get Matched

We carefully pair you based on your goals, personality, schedule, and areas of interest — not just your major.

🚀

4. Start Growing

Your first meeting happens within one week of matching. From there, you set the pace together.

🧭 Mentor Application

All fields are required. Applications reviewed within 5 business days.

🌱 Mentee Application

Open to all students regardless of year or GPA. Be honest — it helps us match you better.
